      How this was built, and how I would build it for any company

      The method
      1. Start from the decision. What does the board need to approve? That sets the page, not the data available.
      2. One headline number. ARR, with its year-on-year growth, and two honesty checks directly under it: net and gross revenue retention (NRR and GRR).
      3. Six insights, each marked good or bad. A reader should know in 15 seconds what is working and what is not.
      4. Separate noise from growth. Say which moves are the natural course of scaling and should revert, and which are not explained by growth.
      5. Four ranked calls to action. Each one is a concrete ask: triggers, data, allocation, funnel fields.
      6. Spend decisions. A payback bar, tranches, and a small ring-fenced bet outside the bar.
      7. Evidence last. Every chart has a table view, secondary views such as ramp sit at the end, and the explorer lets the board test the assumptions.
      What I would need from a real company
      • Monthly ARR by customer, with product, start date and expansion, contraction and churn tagged, so NRR and GRR can be cut by cohort age and product.
      • S&M, COGS and revenue by quarter, ideally split by revenue type.
      • Opportunity data with stage dates, size, owner and lead source.
      • A set of trigger thresholds the exec team agrees in advance.
